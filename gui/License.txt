* reVoice ver. 1.00 *


OVERVIEW.

reVoice ver. 1.00 is a realtime environment 
to synthesize human voice, giving the possibility
to modify the original pitch without altering 
the formant structure of the source.
The program can also produce harmonization effects
and is based on synchronous granular synthesis 
and on other complex techniques to detect
the pitch of a signal in realtime

TECHNICAL INFORMATION.

The program is written in C++ and is based on the the 
PortAudio library (www.portaudio.com) for the realtime engine,
on the FOX library (www.fox-toolkit.org) for the GUI and on the
Pandora Audio framework (www.cryptosound.org) for the generation 
algorithms. This package is made of different programs intended to work
together; for this reason please KEEP ALL THE PACKAGE TOGETHER,
